CONNECTIVITY & TRANSIT GUIDE

How to Reach Hotel Mohan Inn

Prime location on NH-58 / NH-334 Delhi-Haridwar Highway at Bahadarabad ensures effortless arrival from Delhi NCR, Dehradun Airport, and Haridwar Junction.

By Road (NH-58 / NH-334)

Haridwar is directly connected to Delhi NCR and northern cities. Regular luxury Volvo and state express buses operate round the clock from ISBT Kashmiri Gate & Anand Vihar. The hotel provides abundant private on-highway parking.

By Train (Haridwar Junction)

Haridwar Junction Railway Station is on the main Northern Railway line, only 10–12 minutes from our front gate. The New Delhi – Haridwar Shatabdi and Jan Shatabdi Express reach here in under 4 hours.

By Air (Jolly Grant Airport)

The nearest domestic airport is Jolly Grant (DED) Dehradun, situated 45 km away, with non-stop flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Ahmedabad. Hotel airport transfers are gladly arranged upon advance request.
