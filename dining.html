OUR HERITAGE & PURITY

A Tradition of Purity & Taste by the Holy River

For generations, pilgrims returning from Har Ki Pauri have sought solace and authentic nourishment at Mohan Ji Poori Wale. Located on NH-58 Bahadarabad, our dining hall brings this age-old culinary devotion into a grand, hygienic 200-seat sanctuary.

Every batch of crispy golden pooris is fried fresh in pure aromatic ghee, accompanied by slow-simmered spiced aloo bhaji and tangy amchoor chutney. Our master halwais prepare warm halwa and hot rabri jalebi daily following traditional Vedic recipes.
