Sacred Haridwar Distance Matrix

Avoid heavy town congestion. Our highway sanctuary allows quick reach to all holy ghats and temples.

Har Ki Pauri

10 MINS (6 KM)

World-famous Brahma Kund ghat for the divine Sandhya Ganga Maha Aarti, morning holy snan, and ancestral pind daan.

Direct Highway E-Rickshaw Available

Mansa Devi Temple

15 MINS (8 KM)

Revered Siddhapeeth atop Bilwa Parvat. Accessible via scenic ropeway cable car (Udan Khatola) or paved walking trail.

Ropeway Ticketing Assistance

Chandi Devi Temple

18 MINS (10 KM)

Ancient temple atop Neel Parvat established by Adi Shankaracharya. Panoramic vistas of the sacred Shivalik mountain ranges.

Cable Car Passes Arranged

Daksheshwar Mahadev

12 MINS (7 KM)

Historic Kankhal temple where King Daksha performed the yajna. Deeply revered shrine of Lord Shiva and Goddess Sati.

Special Somwar Darshan Cabs

Shantikunj Gayatri Teerth

18 MINS (11 KM)

World headquarters of All World Gayatri Pariwar, offering spiritual meditation, yajna shala, and herbal medicine gardens.

Spiritual Retreat Coordination

Patanjali Yogpeeth

8 MINS (5 KM)

Premier ayurvedic health and yoga institute located conveniently down the same NH-58 expressway corridor.

Health Treatment Base Stay
