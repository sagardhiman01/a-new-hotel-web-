# Hotel Mohan Inn — Luxury Rooms, Banquet & Mohan Ji Poori Wale

A luxury web application created for **Hotel Mohan Inn (Haridwar)** featuring bespoke aesthetics, fluid scroll physics, and typography inspired by modern high-end architectural design systems.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Design Architecture

### 1. Modern Sacred Luxury (Bhagwa & Antique Gold) Palette & Typography
- **Primary Accent (CTA)**: Sacred Light Bhagwa / Luminous Kesariya (`#FF7A28`, `#E66519`)
- **Secondary Accent**: Antique Gold (`#D4AF37`, `#E8CF6B`, `#F4E8B8`)
- **Background**: Soft Cream (`#F9F8F6`, `#F3EFE8`)
- **Surface / Cards**: Warm Sand (`#EFECE6`, `#ECE7DE`)
- **Primary Text**: Deep Charcoal (`#2C2C2C`, `#1A1A1A`)
- **Foundation / Topbar / Footer**: Deep Temple Teak (`#3A1405`)
- **Primary Display Serif**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, Italic) for regal headlines and luxury badges.
- **Modern Sans**: `Inter` for crisp body copy and high legibility.
- **Monospaced Eyebrows & Specs**: `JetBrains Mono` for coordinates, tags, room IDs, and metric data.

---

### 2. World-Class Interactive Modules & Animations
1. **Divine Golden Embers & Floating Diyas Canvas**:
   - Hardware-accelerated canvas animation simulating gentle floating oil lamps and sacred Ganga embers.
2. **Kinetic Scroll Progress Beam**:
   - Thin gold shimmer indicator fixed at top tracking reading progress.
4. **Haridwar Aura & Lighting Studio (Sunny Sanitations inspired)**:
   - Interactive light calibration sandbox with 4 time presets:
     * **Brahma Muhurta (04:30 AM)** — Mystic cool morning mist & silent Ganges dawn.
     * **Surya Namaskar (10:00 AM)** — Radiant golden mountain sunlight.
     * **Sandhya Maha Aarti (06:30 PM)** — Deep amber sacred lamps & temple glow.
     * **Shanti Midnight (11:00 PM)** — Obsidian calm & soft interior LED accents.
     * Sliders for light azimuth, sun elevation, and warmth.
5. **Virtual Suite Discovery Blueprint**:
   - Interactive 360° suite layout with 5 pulsating coordinate pins (`+`). Clicking any pin animates the architectural specification card with real-time durability & luxury radar bars.
6. **Mohan Ji Poori Wale Heritage Restaurant (200 Pax AC)**:
   - Authentic showcase of Haridwar's beloved pure vegetarian breakfast & dinner feasts (Bedmi Poori, Aloo Sabzi, Kesar Halwa, Shahi Thali).
7. **150 Pax AC Banquet Hall & Instant Event Estimator**:
   - Guest slider (25–150 pax) and occasion selector (Wedding, Reception, Corporate, Katha) with instant package pricing and 1-click WhatsApp quote builder.
8. **Curated Photo Gallery & Fullscreen Lightbox (16 High-Res Photos)**:
   - Official images downloaded from `hotelmohaninn.com` showcasing suites, rooms, campus garden, banquet ballroom, Mohan Ji Poori Wale restaurant dishes, and reception lobby.
   - Interactive category tabs (All, Suites & Rooms, Facade & Campus, Mohan Ji Poori Wale, Banquet & Aarti).
   - Fullscreen Lightbox Modal with keyboard arrow navigation and image counter.
9. **Published Tariffs & Bespoke Stay Configurator**:
   - Standard AC (₹4,000), Deluxe AC (₹5,000), Family Suite (₹8,000).
   - Real-time room + meal plan (EP/CP/MAP) + VIP Aarti / Char Dham cab calculation.
   - Formats a pre-filled direct WhatsApp message to MD Manish Mishra (+91 9259368869) or opens the online reservation engine.
9. **Char Dham Yatra & Local Distance Matrix**:
   - Distance & time radar to Har Ki Pauri (10 mins), Mansa Devi (12 mins), Chandi Devi (15 mins), Patanjali Yogpeeth (12 mins), Rishikesh (35 mins).
10. **Web Audio Divine Flute Synthesizer**:
    - Native browser synthesizer playing Raag Bhupali pentatonic flute tones and temple bell harmonics without requiring external MP3 files.
11. **Live Aarti Countdown**:
    - Real-time countdown to Haridwar Sandhya Maha Aarti at Har Ki Pauri Ghat.
12. **Quick Booking Bar & Luxury Modal**:
    - Sticky floating dock at the bottom of the screen with quick reservation trigger.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can launch the web application with Node:
```bash
node server.js
```
Then open your browser at:
```
http://localhost:3005
```
